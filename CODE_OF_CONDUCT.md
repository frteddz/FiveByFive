# Code of conduct

This project is a small fork of [lingdojo/kana-dojo](https://github.com/lingdojo/kana-dojo). Most people here are trying to learn something or fix a small thing and move on. Keep that easy.

## The short version

Be decent to other people. Assume they are trying to help. Critique the change, not the person. If someone is new, explain instead of dismissing.

## What that looks like

- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- No publishing someone else's private information.
- No sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention.
- No trolling, deliberate disruption or endless arguments about settled points.
- Respect different levels of experience. A question that seems basic is allowed.
- Give credit where it is due. The upstream engine and UI belong to the kana-dojo contributors.

## Disagreements

Technical disagreement is normal. Make your case with a concrete reason, accept a decision once it is made, and move on. If a thread is going in circles, ask the maintainer to close it.

## Reporting a problem

If you see behavior that breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile on [github.com/frteddz/FiveByFive](https://github.com/frteddz/FiveByFive). Include what happened and a link to the thread or commit. Reports are handled quietly. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block accounts that keep breaking these rules. The goal is to keep the project usable, not to build a courtroom.
